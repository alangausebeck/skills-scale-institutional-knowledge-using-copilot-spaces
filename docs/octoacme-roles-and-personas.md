# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors are executive stakeholders who provide project funding, remove organizational blockers, and ensure alignment with business strategy. They serve as the ultimate decision-maker and advocate for the project at the leadership level.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project funding and resource allocation
- Make high-level strategic and scope decisions
- Remove organizational barriers and blockers
- Ensure project alignment with business objectives
- Validate project outcomes against business goals
- Escalation point for critical decisions

### Goals
- Maximize return on investment (ROI)
- Ensure strategic alignment across the organization
- Enable timely project delivery with adequate resources
- Reduce escalation cycle time

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Works on escalations and major scope/budget changes
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on business value and strategic priorities
- **Developers**: Provides context on business importance and urgency

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or quarterly executive steering meetings
- Decision approvals on major changes
- Executive summary reports on project health and ROI

---

## Scrum Master / Process Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Process Coaches facilitate Agile practices, remove team impediments, and coach the team on continuous improvement. They ensure adherence to process frameworks and enable the team to work efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove team impediments and blockers
- Coach team members on Agile principles and practices
- Track and report on team velocity and process metrics
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement culture
- Shield team from external distractions

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and productivity
- Improve process efficiency and quality
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team
- Reduce cycle time and rework

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Collaborates on timeline and resource management
- **Developers**: Works closely on daily impediment removal and team health
- **Product Manager**: Helps clarify requirements and manage backlog flow
- **Project Sponsor**: Escalates organizational blockers

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Impediment tracking and escalation
- Retrospective action items and process improvements
- Team metrics and velocity trends

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business needs and technical solutions. They gather, clarify, and document requirements, translate business problems into technical specifications, and help validate solutions meet business expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and gather business requirements
- Document and clarify requirements for development teams
- Create acceptance criteria and test scenarios
- Translate business logic into technical specifications
- Validate solutions against business requirements
- Support user acceptance testing (UAT)

### Goals
- Ensure business needs are accurately translated to technical solutions
- Reduce requirement misunderstandings and rework
- Enable faster time-to-value
- Improve product quality and usability

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Product Manager**: Partners on requirement prioritization and backlog management
- **Developers**: Provides detailed specifications and clarifies requirements during implementation
- **QA Lead**: Collaborates on acceptance criteria and test coverage
- **Stakeholder Representative**: Validates requirements with end-users

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and specifications
- Design review meetings
- Clarification sessions with stakeholders and developers
- UAT coordination and sign-off

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads ensure product quality through comprehensive testing strategies, quality standards, and defect management. They establish quality expectations and measure product readiness for release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain testing strategies and test plans
- Design test cases and ensure adequate test coverage
- Track, prioritize, and manage defects
- Report on quality metrics and product readiness
- Establish and enforce quality standards
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria and definition of done

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality products that meet customer expectations
- Minimize production defects and support incidents
- Reduce testing cycle time through automation and efficiency
- Build quality into the product from the start

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Works on quality standards, test automation, and defect resolution
- **Project Manager**: Reports on quality status and impacts to timeline
- **Business Analyst**: Collaborates on acceptance criteria and requirements validation
- **Product Manager**: Validates product meets user expectations and quality bar

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Quality metrics and defect reports
- Sprint reviews and release readiness assessments
- Quality standards and best practices guidance

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical direction and leadership, ensuring system designs align with organizational standards, scalability requirements, and technical best practices. They guide technical decisions and help identify and mitigate technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate technical vision and standards
- Review system designs and architectural decisions
- Assess technical feasibility and risks
- Recommend technology choices and patterns
- Mentor developers on technical best practices
- Evaluate technical debt and scalability concerns

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable, and resilient systems
- Align technology decisions with business objectives
- Reduce technical risk and technical debt
- Enable team growth through knowledge sharing

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Advises on architecture, design patterns, and technical best practices
- **Project Manager**: Partners on technical feasibility and risk assessment
- **Project Sponsor**: Communicates on major technical decisions with business impact
- **QA Lead**: Collaborates on testability and performance requirements

### Typical Communication
- Architectural design documents and RFCs
- Technical decision logs
- Code and design reviews
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans

---

## Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Representatives serve as the voice of end-users or business stakeholders within the project team. They provide user perspective, validate that solutions meet real-world needs, and ensure alignment between the project deliverables and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Collect and communicate end-user feedback
- Validate proposed solutions against user needs
- Assess impact of changes on end-users
- Participate in acceptance testing and UAT
- Escalate user concerns and issues
- Help prioritize features based on user value

### Goals
- Ensure user needs are understood and addressed
- Reduce post-release issues and user dissatisfaction
- Improve user adoption and product utilization
- Bridge the gap between development and business stakeholders

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Product Manager**: Provides user feedback for prioritization and roadmap decisions
- **Business Analyst**: Collaborates on requirements validation from user perspective
- **Developers**: Clarifies user needs and provides feedback on implementation
- **QA Lead**: Participates in UAT and helps define user-facing acceptance criteria

### Typical Communication
- User feedback summaries and insights
- User acceptance testing participation
- Change impact assessments from user perspective
- User journey documentation and scenarios

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When creating Spaces, assign personas to team members to create realistic project scenarios with clear role responsibilities.
- Reference the interaction patterns between personas to demonstrate how different roles collaborate in project delivery.
